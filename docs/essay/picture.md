# 图画作文

<span class="status-reviewing">reviewing · 首轮待双作者审核</span>

适用于单幅漫画、双幅漫画和带文字说明的图画题。首段要从动作关系、对比关系或文字说明中提炼主题，不能逐项翻译画面。

## 主模板

```text
The picture presents [a person / several people] who are [doing something].
What is particularly striking is that [a key detail, contrast or caption].
Although the drawing is simple, it conveys a meaningful message:
[核心主题] plays an important role in [个人成长 / social development / the realization of one's goals].

The message conveyed by the picture deserves serious consideration.
For one thing, [核心主题] enables individuals to [积极作用一].
It also helps them to [积极作用二], especially when they are faced with
[困难、压力或现实挑战]. For another, the absence of [核心主题]
may lead to [消极后果]. This is because [原因或机制].

In my view, [核心主题] should be valued and practiced in everyday life.
Individuals are expected to [个人行动]. At the same time, schools,
families and relevant institutions should [公共支持].
Only when personal effort is combined with a supportive environment
can [积极结果].
```

## 三种入口

### 正面品质

使用 `enables`, `helps`, `supports`, `strengthens` 等表达，主体段强调价值、作用和长期收益。

### 负面现象

```text
The picture exposes a disturbing phenomenon: [负面现象].
Through this seemingly simple scene, the cartoonist warns us that
[问题本质或潜在危害].
```

主体段再写原因和后果，结尾同时写个人自律与制度、教育或监管。

### 双图对比

```text
The two pictures present two contrasting situations.
In the first picture, [描述第一幅图]. By contrast, [描述第二幅图].
The comparison suggests that [中心观点].
```

## 解读重点

- 描述对象以人物、动作、关系和文字说明为主。
- 寓意必须来自画面证据，不能凭空拔高。
- 第二段至少解释一个“为什么”，而不是只列两个好处。
- 不要每道题都使用相同的“社会媒体、政府、个人”三元结尾。

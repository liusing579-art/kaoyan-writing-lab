# 材料作文

<span class="status-draft">draft · 需要独立材料题测试集</span>

> 需要直接背诵和替换的完整版本，请先看[四类大作文模板总表](/essay/template-pack)。

材料作文的首要任务是准确概括材料并提炼立场。不能直接套用图画作文的 `The picture presents...`，也不能把材料中的个案扩展成普遍事实。

## 首段：概括与立意

```text
The material tells us that [概括材料中的人物、事件或观点].
What deserves particular attention is that [材料中的关键转折、矛盾或结论].
The material therefore raises an important question: [核心问题].
In my view, [中心观点].
```

名言型材料可使用：

```text
The saying that “[名言或核心观点]” may appear simple,
but it carries a profound implication.
It reminds us that [准确释义].
```

## 第二段：论证成立条件

```text
The importance of this idea can be understood from several aspects.
First, [理由一]. It enables people to [具体作用].
Second, [理由二]. When individuals are faced with [现实困难],
this principle helps them to [具体结果].
In addition, the material is relevant to society as a whole,
because [社会层面的意义].
```

## 第三段：转化为行动

```text
Therefore, the lesson of the material should be translated into action.
For individuals, it is important to [个人行动].
For schools, families and society, more should be done to [公共支持].
As long as we [核心行动], we will be better prepared to [积极结果].
```

## 当前限制

这一页暂不标记为 `verified`。项目需要建立至少三道材料型模拟题，分别测试名言型、故事型和现象型材料，再决定是否拆分为三个专用入口。
